Behaviour as the GPUI app's window_lifecycle.rs: each window comes back where it was (fit to the displays by the core's rules), one that was in the Dock opens behind,
unfocused, and goes back there (it is never the one that comes forward: `front_after_restore`; before this the shell activated every window as it opened), the one
that was in front is in front. A window opened in another profile (Open Window in) is saved in that profile's database (W06.txt). Columns, groups, splits and the selection
are saved per window by the core (record_of / window_record) and were restored live earlier (R-V). A window moved out of the sidebar by tear-off (S07.txt) is saved at the place it opened.
Not run: a saved full-screen or zoomed window coming back as such (WindowFrames.settle, as before), a display that is gone (core fit_frame has a test), the Dock itself.
