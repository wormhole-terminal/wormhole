# Agent logos

Monochrome logos shown next to terminals in the sidebar and in Settings → Coding
agents. They are drawn tinted with the theme's text color.

Most files come unmodified (only renamed) from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org)
(`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@<version>/icons/<slug>.svg`; the version is in the
table). Simple Icons releases its SVGs under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md);
where an icon lists its own license, that one applies instead.

The ones Simple Icons doesn't have come from [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons)
v1.101.0 (`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@lobehub/icons-static-svg@1.101.0/icons/<name>.svg`, the
monochrome variant), MIT licensed (below). Only the root element was changed: its `1em` width and
height, inline style and `currentColor` fill were taken out so it is sized and tinted like the
others; the paths and the fill rule are as drawn.

The logos are trademarks of their owners, used only to name the product they stand for — no
affiliation or endorsement is implied (see Simple Icons'
[disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md)).
