Montages from a `--background` instance (data dir /tmp/Wormhole-swift-s3-themes-motion-a), driven through the control folder
(`click`, `window-shot`, `increase-contrast on|off`, `reduce-motion on|off`, `set icon_animations=false`). `transcript.txt` is the
instance's out.txt. Each montage is left to right (or row by row) rest, then frames of the run.

- plus-quarter-turn.png: rest, mid-turn (about 60 degrees), after. gate-off-reduce-on.png: the same click with Animate icons off,
  with Reduce Motion on, and with both allowing: only the last turns.
- split-fill.png: Split right filling its right half over the three frames, Split down filling its bottom half.
- tab-glyphs-1/2.png: history's dot with the lines still to draw, notes' page before its lines, changes' circles filled.
- refresh-spin.png: refresh arrows mid-run (two frames) and at rest.
- title-line-ic.png: the title row without and with Increase Contrast (a 2 pt line in the group's colour under the bar).
- theme-cards-ic.png, git-preview-ic.png: Settings theme cards and the git preview card without and with Increase Contrast.
